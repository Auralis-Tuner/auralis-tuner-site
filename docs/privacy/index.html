PRIVACY POLICY

Private by default.

Effective July 13, 2026

Overview

The Auralis Tuner app works without an account and does not collect personal data. This policy separately explains the app and the public website that hosts this page.

The app: microphone access

Auralis Tuner uses microphone input only to analyze pitch in real time. Audio is processed on your device. It is not recorded, saved, transmitted, or uploaded.

The app: data collection

Auralis Tuner does not collect personal information, analytics, diagnostics, location, contacts, identifiers, usage history, or advertising data through the app. It does not track you across apps or websites.

The app: local preferences

Your tuner preferences, such as note-name format, reference pitch, detection sensitivity, and selected preset, may be stored locally on your device using Apple system storage. These preferences are not sent to us.

The app: network and third parties

The app does not require a network connection for tuning and does not integrate advertising networks, analytics services, or third-party tracking SDKs.

This website: GitHub Pages hosting

This public website is hosted by GitHub Pages. When you visit it, GitHub may process your IP address and necessary technical or website-usage logs under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Auralis Tuner does not use analytics or cookies on this website and does not receive or access GitHub’s hosting logs.

Children

Because Auralis Tuner does not collect personal data through the app or this website, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. GitHub’s handling of necessary hosting data is governed by its own privacy statement.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version and effective date will be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email auralistuner@icloud.com.

概述

Auralis Tuner App 无需账号，也不会收集个人数据。本政策分别说明 App 以及承载本页面的公开网站。

App：麦克风权限

Auralis Tuner 仅使用麦克风输入进行实时音高分析。音频只在设备本地处理，不会被录音、保存、传输或上传。

App：数据收集

Auralis Tuner App 不收集个人信息、分析数据、诊断数据、位置、联系人、设备标识符、使用记录或广告数据，也不会跨 App 或网站跟踪用户。

App：本地偏好

音名格式、基准音高、检测灵敏度与乐器预设等偏好可能通过 Apple 系统存储保存在设备本地，不会发送给我们。

App：网络与第三方

调音功能无需联网，App 不包含广告网络、分析服务或第三方跟踪 SDK。

本网站：GitHub Pages 托管

本公开网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。访问本网站时，GitHub 可能会根据其《GitHub 一般隐私声明》处理访问者的 IP 地址以及必要的技术日志或网站使用日志。Auralis Tuner 不在本网站使用分析工具或 Cookie，也不会接收或访问 GitHub 的托管日志。

儿童隐私

由于 Auralis Tuner 不通过 App 或本网站收集个人数据，我们不会有意收集儿童的个人数据。GitHub 对必要托管数据的处理受其自身隐私声明约束。

政策更新

如果本政策发生变化，更新后的内容与生效日期会发布在本页面。

联系
