How can we help?

Quick answers for microphone access, pitch detection, and tuning settings on iPhone, iPad, and Mac.

Email support: auralistuner@icloud.com

Getting started

  1. Open Auralis Tuner and allow microphone access.
  2. Tap or click Start Listening.
  3. Play a clear, sustained note near the microphone.
  4. Use the precision dial or strobe to bring the reading to the center.

No pitch or waveform appears

  • Confirm microphone permission in Settings or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone.
  • Play one steady note at a moderate volume and move closer to the microphone.
  • Reduce background noise and mute other audio apps.
  • Check the selected instrument preset and detection sensitivity in Settings.
  • After changing the audio input device, stop listening and start again.

Note names and calibration

Open Settings to choose letter names, fixed Do Re Mi, or numbered notation (1 2 3 with 1=C). You can adjust the A4 reference from 420 to 460 Hz.

Privacy and connectivity

Auralis Tuner does not require an account or internet connection for tuning. Microphone audio is analyzed only on your device and is never recorded, saved, or uploaded.

如何获取帮助？

这里提供 iPhone、iPad 和 Mac 版 Auralis Tuner 的麦克风权限、音高检测与调音设置说明。

支持邮箱：auralistuner@icloud.com

快速使用

  1. 打开 Auralis Tuner，并允许麦克风访问。
  2. 点击“开始监听”。
  3. 在麦克风附近演奏一个清晰、持续的音。
  4. 根据精密指针或频闪画面，将读数调到中心。

没有出现音高或波形

  • 在“设置”或“系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 麦克风”中确认麦克风权限。
  • 以适中音量演奏稳定的单音，并靠近麦克风。
  • 减少环境噪音，并将其他音频 App 静音。
  • 在设置中检查乐器预设与检测灵敏度。
  • 切换音频输入设备后，停止监听并重新开始。

音名与校准

在设置中可选择字母音名、固定唱名 Do Re Mi，或简谱音名（1 2 3，1=C）。A4 基准音可在 420 至 460 Hz 之间调整。

隐私与联网

Auralis Tuner 调音时无需账号或网络连接。麦克风音频只在设备本地分析，绝不会被录音、保存或上传。
